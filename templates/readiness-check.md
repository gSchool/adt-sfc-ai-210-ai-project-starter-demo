# Readiness Check: <project or task area>

**Reach for this when tasks keep producing weak results and you suspect the inputs rather
than the prompt.** Once per project, or when something changes — not per task.

Four dimensions. Each gets a rating and **one sentence of specific, observable evidence.**
"Constrained because ticket categories differ across regions" is evidence. "Constrained" is
not.

**Ready Now** — you could ship a credible version without heroics.
**Constrained** — usable if you limit scope and fix named gaps. Most real work lands here.
**Not Ready** — do not proceed until prerequisites are addressed.
